## 2. Chart-Choice Justification

### Question 1 — Which month had the highest total revenue?

**Chart: Column chart (bar chart).**

This is a univariate comparison because the focus is total revenue across one categorical dimension, month. A column chart makes the three monthly values easy to compare within 10 seconds, and the y-axis should start at zero so the visual comparison is not misleading. No legend is needed because there is only one revenue series.

### Question 2 — What percentage share does Ethnic Wear represent of April's total revenue?

**Chart: Pie chart.**

This is a part-to-whole question involving one category's share of a single month's total revenue. A pie chart can communicate the 24.92% share quickly, provided the chart remains simple and readable. A legend is unnecessary if the category is directly labeled, and 3D effects should be avoided.

### Question 3 — How do the four regions compare on total revenue?

**Chart: Column chart (bar chart).**

This is a univariate comparison of total revenue across the categorical variable region. A column chart allows North, South, East, and West to be compared quickly, with the y-axis starting at zero. There is only one revenue series, so a legend is not required.
